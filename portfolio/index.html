Europe Day × ONCE

I designed ONCE's coupon dedicated to Europe Day in May 2023, centred on the European Year of Skills. The design aimed to reflect a diverse European society in which everyone has a place, while giving visibility to people facing greater difficulties in developing their skills. The coupon was presented during a Europe Day event featuring discussions on inclusion and the future of the European Union.
